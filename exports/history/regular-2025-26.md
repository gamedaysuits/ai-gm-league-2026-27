## Method

- Stats: NHL stats REST season summaries (skater/summary, isGame=false), seasonId=20252026, gameTypeId=2, by player ID. As a cross-check, the 49,998 per-game lines of all 1,312 regular-season games were summed per player and compared with these totals: 0 mismatches.
- Drafted players are matched to NHL player IDs by name and club, using the NHL's 2025-26 stats. Club shows every NHL club the player played for in 2025-26.
- GM names are the labels in the draft file. ChatGPT5 was actually GPT-4o, and MistralLarge was Mistral Small 3.1.
- One row of the source file (Drake Batherson) has a stray extra field, so DraftedBy and Price are read as each row's last two fields.
- Name notes:
  - Elias Pettersson: the forward, Elias Pettersson (8480012, C). A defenceman with the same name also played for Vancouver in 2025-26.
  - JJ Peterka is John-Jason Peterka (8482175) in NHL data.
